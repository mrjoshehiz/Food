# ILÉ logo motion
## Style Prompt
A confident Lagos restaurant wordmark. Tomato red typography assembles with a measured stagger, the acute accent settles above the E, and two fine steam curves drift once. Finish on a quiet readable logo, with no loading gate.
## Colors
- #ffffff canvas, matching the existing approved app
- #d92d20 wordmark and steam
- #171717 supporting ink
- #fff1ef decorative warmth
## Typography
Italiana wordmark, Manrope supporting text. Self-hosted fonts.
## Motion
One 3.2-second reveal: three distinct easing curves, then a still hold. Reduced-motion users receive the completed frame. No perpetual pulsing or flashing.
## What NOT to Do
No neon, gradients, sound, forced waiting, fake 3D, or bouncing buttons.
